## EPIC formalism and calibration

**Thyagarajan, N., Beardsley, A. P., Bowman, J. D., & Morales, M. F. (2017).** *A generic and efficient E-field Parallel Imaging Correlator for next-generation radio telescopes.* MNRAS 467, 715. [arXiv:1510.08318](https://arxiv.org/abs/1510.08318) · [doi:10.1093/mnras/stx113](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stx113)

This paper does four things:

- derives the EPIC imaging equation;
- validates EPIC against visibility-based holographic imaging, in simulation and on LWA1 data;
- treats telescopes composed of non-identical antennas;
- presents a cost and data-rate comparison with FX correlation across existing and proposed radio telescopes.

**Beardsley, A. P., Thyagarajan, N., Bowman, J. D., & Morales, M. F. (2017).** *An efficient feedback calibration algorithm for direct imaging radio telescopes.* MNRAS 470, 4720. [arXiv:1603.02126](https://arxiv.org/abs/1603.02126) · [doi:10.1093/mnras/stx1512](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stx1512)

EPICal: real-time calibration by correlation of antenna voltages with image pixels, at a cost of $\mathcal{O}(N_a)$. A 65% increase in dynamic range was obtained on LWA data.

## The LWA-SV imager

**Kent, J., Dowell, J., Beardsley, A., Thyagarajan, N., Taylor, G., & Bowman, J. (2019).** *A real-time, all-sky, high time resolution, direct imager for the Long Wavelength Array.* MNRAS 486, 5052. [arXiv:1904.11422](https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.11422) · [doi:10.1093/mnras/stz1206](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stz1206)

The first real-time GPU implementation of EPIC, within Bifrost at LWA-SV. The paper presents the pipeline design, benchmarks, a roofline analysis establishing the memory-bandwidth limit, and the detection of a meteor reflection at 50 ms cadence.

**Kent, J., et al. (2020).** *Direct wide-field radio imaging in real-time at high time resolution using antenna electric fields.* MNRAS 491, 254. [arXiv:1909.03973](https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.03973) · [doi:10.1093/mnras/stz3028](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stz3028)

Direct imaging by direct Fourier transform, with exact wide-field and non-coplanar correction, at a cost comparable to gridding and FFT at the scale of LWA-SV.

**Krishnan, H., Kent, J., Dowell, J., et al. (2020).** *Development of an optimized real-time radio transient imager for LWA-SV.* USNC-URSI National Radio Science Meeting, 169. [doi:10.23919/USNC/URSI49741.2020.9321611](https://doi.org/10.23919/USNC/URSI49741.2020.9321611)

Restructuring of the convolutional gridding kernel for GPU execution.

**Krishnan, H., Beardsley, A. P., Bowman, J. D., Dowell, J., Kolopanis, M., Taylor, G. B., & Thyagarajan, N. (2023).** *Optimization and commissioning of the EPIC commensal radio transient imager for the Long Wavelength Array.* MNRAS 520, 1928. [arXiv:2301.09662](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.09662) · [doi:10.1093/mnras/stad263](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stad263)

This paper reports:

- the hardware upgrade and the optimized gridding and multiplication kernels, giving 1.8 MHz per GPU at 64²;
- commissioning as a commensal back end;
- validation on solar Type III bursts, pulsars, a giant pulse from PSR B1133+16, and ionospheric scintillation.

**Reddy, K., Bowman, J. D., Beardsley, A. P., Taylor, G. B., Dowell, J., & Taylor, C. (2023).** *An optimized GPU kernel for real-time radio imaging.* Proc. IEEE HPCC/DSS/SmartCity/DependSys, 523. [doi:10.1109/HPCC-DSS-SmartCity-DependSys60770.2023.00077](https://doi.org/10.1109/HPCC-DSS-SmartCity-DependSys60770.2023.00077)

A fused, on-chip, half-precision imaging kernel. It produces 128² images in four polarization products for 128 channels per RTX 4090, in real time.

**Reddy, K., Bowman, J. D., Dowell, J., Taylor, G. B., Beardsley, A. P., & Taylor, C. (2024).** *Architecture and implementation of a 25000 FPS radio camera on the Long Wavelength Array.* Proc. SPIE 13101, 131011C. [doi:10.1117/12.3012524](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.3012524)

The six-node system that images 19.2 MHz at 80 ms cadence. The paper covers orchestration, alert-driven source monitoring, the pixel-spectrogram database, image archiving, video streaming and the web interface.

## Precursors

**Tegmark, M., & Zaldarriaga, M. (2009).** *The Fast Fourier Transform Telescope.* Phys. Rev. D 79, 083530. [arXiv:0805.4414](https://arxiv.org/abs/0805.4414)

Correlation by spatial FFT for identical antennas on a regular grid.

**Tegmark, M., & Zaldarriaga, M. (2010).** *Omniscopes: Large area telescope arrays with only N log N computational cost.* Phys. Rev. D 82, 103501. [arXiv:0909.0001](https://arxiv.org/abs/0909.0001)

Extension of the FFT telescope to hierarchical grid layouts.

**Morales, M. F. (2011).** *Enabling next-generation dark energy and epoch of reionization radio observatories with the MOFF correlator.* PASP 123, 1265. [arXiv:0812.3669](https://arxiv.org/abs/0812.3669)

The MOFF correlator: direct imaging for arbitrary antenna layouts and types, equivalent to optimal visibility-based imaging. The paper also proposes feedback calibration.

## Related work

**Thyagarajan, N. (2025).** *Comparison of fast, hybrid imaging architectures for multi-scale, hierarchical aperture arrays.* PASA 42, e001. [arXiv:2411.17804](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.17804)

A cost comparison of beamforming, EPIC, FX and hybrid architectures for hierarchical aperture arrays.

**Thyagarajan, N., Thekkeppattu, J. N., & Humphrey, A. (2026).** *A fast, wide-field, and real-time imaging prototype for large aperture arrays.* Proc. SPIE 14153. [arXiv:2609.03027](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03027)

An implementation of EPIC on an FPGA platform (AMD Alveo V80), evaluated in simulation.
