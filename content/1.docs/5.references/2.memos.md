## Recommended reading

The memos may be read in the following order:

1. **Memo 001**, for the motivation and the scope of the direct-imaging programme.
2. **Memos 007, 008 and 012**, for the characteristics and validation of EPIC data.
3. **Memo 009**, for the design of the GPU implementation.
4. **Memos 010 and 013**, for the data management and its use in transient searches.

## Open issues

The memos and papers identify the following unresolved issues, which bear on the interpretation of EPIC data:

- **Calibration.** No real-time antenna gain or bandpass calibration is applied, and image values are in arbitrary units (see [Calibration](/docs/theory/calibration)).
- **Astrometry.** A residual image rotation of approximately 1°–3°, increasing toward the horizon, was measured relative to the FX imager (Memo 012).
- **Interference.** No online flagging is performed. Interference near the horizon couples into the full image through the sidelobes of the point-spread function (Memos 002, 013).
- **Commensal tuning.** The observing frequency is set by the station schedule, and imaging sessions terminate on retuning (Memo 007).
- **Transient processing.** Dedispersion, automated detection and bandpass normalization are not part of the real-time system (Memo 013).
